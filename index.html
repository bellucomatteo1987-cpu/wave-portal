Frequently Asked Questions

Can this tool touch my other funds?

No. Connecting your wallet only allows the tool to scan what assets you hold. You retain full control and must manually sign to burn only the specific junk tokens you selected.

What happens when I "burn" a token?

The token storage account is permanently wiped from the blockchain, destroying the junk asset and triggering an immediate refund of the network rent fees back into your balance.
